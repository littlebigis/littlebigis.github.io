IDK why I do the things I do

Fucked up yet again. I think I have either gotten too good at compartmentalizing to the point where I am legitimately 2 people, or else I am an actual sociopath. As a disclaimer, I have not broken any laws or physically hurt anybody.

There is the normal, everyday me that all of my friends and family knows. I go to work, I do my normal little everyday tasks, and I honor my obligations. Then there's the me that only me knows. The part of me that gives into my desires, even though I know I shouldn't.

The normal, everyday me knows that I should be embarassed by the things that the secret me does. I am acutely aware of the concept of a conscience. But the secret me doesn't feel bad at all. At most, I will feel a little bit of anxiety. But it is anxiety about getting caught and having to deal with the consequences of my actions. I do not feel remorse or regret about the things I do. I do not feel guilty for how my actions could hurt others, because unless I get found out, people in my personal life don't know what I have been doing.

I have been working on myself. I am a Buddhist, and I have been trying to practice the Dharma better. But I am so inconsistent with things. I go through cycles. I will practice well, but then I will give into my desires and conduct myself unethically. I feel like I really struggle with the core concept of Buddhism, The Middle Way. I seem to gravitate towards extremes. Either very devout practice, or pretty severe "sin".

I think part of my struggle with being a good Buddhist and in turn a good person, is in part my inability to be accepted by the people in my life as a Buddhist. I live in an area that is not historically Buddhist, many people are or at least were Christian or Catholic. Buddhism is seem as "new age" and I feel like if I told the people in my life that I am Buddhist, at worst they would make fun of me, but at the best they would just not take it seriously. This kind of holds me back. I am not a Buddhist because I am seeking the attention or approval of others, but is it also discouraging to feel like I need to hide my practice from others.
