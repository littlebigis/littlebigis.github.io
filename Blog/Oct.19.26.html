Reading

I am learning the importance of literacy in today's world. Specifically in the realms of education and reading for enjoyment.

In terms of education, specifically elementary education, literacy is the foundation upon which all other learning is built upon. If a student cannot ready at grade level from a young age, it becomes more difficult to learn in nearly every other subject simply because reading is the primary medium to take in information. Besides simply being able to read the words on the page, reading comprehension is an invaluable skill that must be developed from a young age if one hopes to become successful in their learning.

Since the pandemic, students have been granted greater access to technology. This nearly unlimited access to tech has resulted in a greatly reduced attention span and apathy when it comes to reading and other basic tasks among young students. These issues have been compounded by the use of AI. Students will fall below grade level in reading, and as they age, the gap between them and their peers grows greater and greater, until it seems nearly impossible for them to catch up. Simply reading to or with your children at home for 15 minutes per day can greatly increase their literacy skills. Pairing reading with limiting their access to technology will build their attention span and reading/ writing stamina.

I would consider the educational crisis that children today are facing to be an emergency. But I also think that it is possible to undo the damage that has been done. Parents need to play an active role in education and discipline at home. They need to stop relying on teachers to be the sole educators and role models in their child's life. Teachers are only able to do so much, especially when the lessons and good habits being taught at school are being undone at home. I know that parents are facing pressures in their lives outside of the home and that they are often burnt out when they get home. I know that many households consist of either a single parent or two working parents. But no matter how difficult the circumstances, parents have a responsibility to their children to provide them with an environment that promotes proper development. It is no longer an option for parents to give their children an iPad or plop them in front of a TV screen in lieu of spending quality time with them.


Now that I have hopped off my "it takes a village" soapbox, I would like to share my thoughts on the importance of reading for pleasure in everyone's life, not just in the lives of those whose brains are not yet fully developed. First, I would life to define what I consider to be reading. In order for reading to be considered reading, the reader must be consuming media that was written with the sole purpose of being read. What I mean by this is, if you are reading text that is contained within some other form of media whose purpose is something other than reading, for example, reading the dialogue in a video game or reading the subtitles for a movie or TikTok video, this is not what I consider to be "reading". So by this definition, the only thing that can be considered reading would be reading a book, magazine, news article, poem, or an electronic version of any of these. Bonus points if you read any of these in print format, because the white light of a screen can impede your sleep, but reading on a screen is better than not reading at all.

Now that we are on the same page about what actually constitutes reading, it is time we discuss the benefits of reading for pleasure. It is one thing to sit down and read a book or article because you have been assigned a particular reading for a class or to accomplish some task at work. I mean, props to you if that's why you are reading! But going out of your way to read something that you actually enjoy, and doing it in your spare time is better. When you are reading for leisure, you strip away the pressures and constraints that are put on yourself by outside forces. This means that you can read at your own pace, and you are more likely to understand what you are reading. When given the option to choose what you are reading, you are also free to leave your comfort zone in a non-judgemental way. If you are curious about a particular subject you can explore it without anyone else knowing. People that read for fun have also been found to be more kind, empathetic, and open to listening to the points of view of others.
