The Middle Way

The Middle Way is a foundational concept in Buddhism that refers to a balanced path of practice and understanding, avoiding all forms of extremes.

The Middle Way rejects both sensual indulgences (luxury and attachment to pleasure) and extreme asceticism (self-mortification and excessive fasting). The Middle Way requires a conscious, ongoing judgment informed by personal experience and mindfulness.

The Middle Way encourages holding views with flexibility so they can adapt to new experiences and insights rather than clinging to absolute dogmas. In its essense The Middle Way emphasizes being able to steer away from extreme indulgence or extreme deprivation and finding a happy medium. It is paired with The Noble Eightfold Path as a way to live life without hurting others.
