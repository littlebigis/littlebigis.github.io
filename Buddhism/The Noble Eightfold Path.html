The Noble Eightfold Path

The Noble Eightfold Path is a practical set of guidelines that the Buddha laid out that if followed, can lead to the cessation of suffering.


Wisdom (Prajñā)

Right View (or Understanding):

Seeing the world as it truly is, specifically understanding the Four Noble Truths, karma, and impermanence.
This also includes not letting gossip, negativity, or the opinions of others interfere with your ability to see things how they actually are.


Right Intention (or Resolve):

Committing to self-improvement and acting from a place of love, non-violence, and detachment rather than greed or anger.


Ethical Conduct (Sīla)

Right Speech

Abstaining from lying, harsh language, malicious gossip, and useless chatter; speaking truthfully and kindly.


Right Action

Behaving morally by avoiding harm to living beings, stealing, and sexual misconduct.


Right Livelihood:

Earning a living in a way that does not exploit or harm others (e.g., avoiding trades in weapons, intoxicants, or slaughtering animals).


Mental Discipline (Samādhi)

Right Effort

Cultivating a positive state of mind by preventing unwholesome thoughts and nurturing wholesome ones.


Right Mindfulness

Developing constant awareness of your body, feelings, mind, and thoughts without judgment.


Right Concentration

Practicing deep, focused meditation to achieve mental clarity and tranquility.
